Job Market Paper

BigTech Credit Market Competition and Bank Portfolio Risk

Draft
Abstract

How does bank lending reacts when BigTech platforms use their proprietary information to compete for their borrowers? To explore this, I develop a model of the credit market where I show that platform entry, by attracting comparatively more safe borrowers, can increase the bank’s portfolio risk. Improvements in platform information strengthen this adverse selection problem, while funding costs shape the extent of competition. Despite deteriorating bank portfolio quality, platform entry can expand productive lending and improve aggregate efficiency. These findings highlight how information-driven competition can have contrasting effects on incumbent banks and aggregate credit allocation.

Working Papers

Media and Political Competition with Rationally Inattentive Voters

with Eva Pérez

Draft
Abstract

We study how the rise of Social Media affects professional news quality, media profits, voter attention, and political belief polarization. Traditional Media produces information about a common policy-relevant state, while Social Media intermediates this content and combines it with personalized information. Voters are rationally inattentive and choose how much additional costly information to acquire. We show that greater Social Media penetration can weaken incentives to invest in professional reporting when the returns from intermediated audiences are sufficiently low. In our baseline calibration, this reduces both reporting quality and Traditional Media's profits. Revenue sharing introduces a strategic trade-off: it encourages professional reporting but weakens Social Media's incentives to transmit that information faithfully. These decisions shape voter attention and the dispersion of political beliefs through personalized information and differences in the quality of common information. Our numerical results illustrate how their interaction can generate non-monotonic effects on reporting quality and belief polarization.
